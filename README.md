        <h2>Hero Heading: “Operator vs Retailer: The Ultimate Liquidity Game”</h2>
        <p>Subheading: Cat & Mouse Game in Forex – Smart Money Concepts 2024-25</p>
        <p>In the world of Forex trading, the battle between operators (institutional players like banks and hedge funds) and retail traders is a classic game of cat and mouse. Operators, armed with vast resources and insider knowledge, manipulate market psychology to trap unsuspecting retailers. This module dives into Smart Money Concepts (SMC) and Inner Circle Trader (ICT) strategies, revealing how operators exploit patterns like Order Blocks, Fair Value Gaps (FVGs), and Liquidity Pools. By understanding this "liquidity game," you can avoid becoming the prey. Let's break it down step by step.</p>
    </header>

    <div class="section">
        <h2>Section 1: Retailers Ab ‘Smart’ Ban Rahe Hain (Retailers Are Trying to Get 'Smart')</h2>
        <img src="https://via.placeholder.com/600x400?text=Retailer+Analyzing+Order+Blocks+%26+FVG" alt="Retailer analyzing Order Blocks & FVG">
        <p>Retail traders are now obsessed with "smart" tools. They scour charts for Order Blocks (areas where large orders were placed), Fair Value Gaps (FVGs – price imbalances), and Liquidity Pools (zones where stop-loss orders cluster). Their thought process: "Operators will crash the market from here, so I'll sell too."</p>
        <p>But here's the problem: When everyone piles into the same trade at one level, it creates a new crowd. This fresh liquidity is exactly what operators crave. They use it to fuel their moves, turning retail "smartness" against them. Retailers think they're following the pros, but they're just feeding the machine.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="section">
        <h2>Section 2: Operator ka Naya Trap – ‘Trap Within the Trap’ (Operator's New Trap – ‘Trap Within the Trap’)</h2>
        <img src="https://via.placeholder.com/600x400?text=Fake+Bearish+Order+Block" alt="Fake Bearish Order Block">
        <p>Operators have evolved beyond old-school traps like obvious support/resistance breaks. Now, they fake SMC/ICT levels to lure retailers.</p>
        <p>Imagine a clear Bearish Order Block (BOB) – a textbook setup that screams "sell." Retailers jump in, placing stops just above. The operator pushes price down to build confidence, then whips it back up, hitting all those stops. The "smart" SMC level becomes the trap itself.</p>
        <p>Lesson: In 2024-25, the patterns you trust are often engineered to exploit you. Operators know which YouTubers and indicators retailers follow, so they craft perfect setups to collect orders and stops before reversing.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="section">
        <h2>Section 3: Liquidity Grabbing 2.0</h2>
        <img src="https://via.placeholder.com/600x400?text=FVG+%26+Mitigation+Block+Example" alt="FVG & Mitigation Block example">
        <p>Operators are getting sophisticated with liquidity grabs:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>FVG Fakes</strong>: They fill an FVG to trigger buys, then slam the price down, trapping longs.</li>
            <li><strong>Mitigation Blocks</strong>: Touching an obvious mitigation block (a zone that "mitigates" imbalances) to hunt stop-losses.</li>
            <li><strong>Institutional Double Fakes</strong>: Breaking higher timeframes (HTF) to lure trades, then trapping everyone back in the range.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>This is liquidity grabbing on steroids – operators create illusions of opportunity to harvest retail capital.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="section">
        <h2>Section 4: Aap Kaise Bache? (How Do You Survive?) – Advanced Psychology</h2>
        <img src="https://via.placeholder.com/600x400?text=Trader+Analyzing+Multiple+Timeframes" alt="Trader patiently analyzing multiple timeframes">
        <p>To beat the game, shift your mindset:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Confluence Over Single Levels</strong>: Don't rely on one SMC pattern. Check weekly/monthly levels, news events, and volume for confirmation.</li>
            <li><strong>Patience is Power</strong>: Levels that look like "free money" are often traps. Wait for the second move – the first is usually fake.</li>
            <li><strong>Think Against the Crowd</strong>: Operators anticipate retail behavior, so do the opposite.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Shield yourself with:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Discipline</strong>: Stick to rules; no emotional deviations.</li>
            <li><strong>Risk Management</strong>: Always use stop-losses and small position sizes.</li>
            <li><strong>Market Context</strong>: Understand the full story, not just patterns.</li>
            <li><strong>Humility</strong>: Operators are always one step ahead – admit you don't know everything.</li>
        </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="section">
        <h2>Section 5: Operator vs Retailer Mindset Breakdown</h2>
